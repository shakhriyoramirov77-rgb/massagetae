About this prototype

The base map is real; the listings on top of it are illustrative. Streets, place names and terrain are live cartography from OpenStreetMap contributors. The 20 named entities, their pin positions, and every demand statistic (population, income, foot traffic, illness rates, workforce, prices, auction countdowns) are fictional examples built to demonstrate the scoring model — grounded in the real character of each district, but not references to specific real properties, companies, or e-auksion.uz listings.

Opportunity Score blends five demand signals — foot traffic & access, purchasing power, population & growth, unmet demand, and category-specific demand (illness rates for clinics/pharmacies, tourist footfall for hotels/restaurants) — weighted differently per building category. USD figures use an illustrative fixed rate (1 USD ≈ 12,700 so'm), not a live exchange rate.
